What this prototype proves

Automation

Expert calibration steps can be transformed into a reproducible computational workflow.

Guardrails

Independent validation, explicit constraints and traceability reduce the risk of accepting a numerically attractive but fragile model.

Extensibility

The calibration harness is designed so the hydrological engine can later be replaced or connected to HEC-HMS.

HEC-HMS integration is a future step — not implemented in this prototype.
